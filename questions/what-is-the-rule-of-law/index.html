What is the rule of law?

Answer. Any of these is accepted.
No one is above the law
  • Also accepted
  • Everyone must follow the law
  • Leaders must obey the law
  • Government must obey the law

Source: U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services, 2025 civics test. Last reviewed September 5, 2026.
