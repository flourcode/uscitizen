The 2008 citizenship test

If you filed Form N-400 before October 20, 2025, this is your version: 100 questions, 10 asked, 6 to pass.

100possible questions
10asked at the interview
6correct to pass

Which test you take is set by your filing date, not your interview date, and it does not change if your interview is delayed. Your N-400 receipt notice shows the date.

The two lists overlap heavily. Nearly every 2008 question appears in the 2025 list in the same or similar wording; the 2025 test added about 28 deeper questions and dropped a few simple ones (like naming a state that borders Canada). If you are on the 2008 test, practicing the 128 will more than cover you, and you need only 6 of 10.

65/20 applicants on the 2008 test study a different set of 20 starred questions from the 2008 list.

The official 2008 list is at uscis.gov/citizenship. USCIS also keeps the current officials for both versions on one page.

Ready when you are.

The 2025 list contains almost everything on the 2008 list.
