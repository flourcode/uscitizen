What you can tell people about it

  • It is free, has no advertising inside the trainer, and asks for no account, email, or personal information. Progress is stored on the person's own phone.
  • Questions and accepted answers are taken from the official USCIS list for the 2025 test and checked against it; current officials are updated after each change.
  • It works on any phone in a browser, and can be added to the home screen like an app.
  • It is independent: not affiliated with USCIS, and not legal advice.

For attorneys specifically

The two pages most useful to your clients are the N-400 questions the officer asks and the interview practice, which asks those questions aloud with a pause to answer. Both tell clients that a "yes" to any "have you ever" question is a reason to call you before the interview, not after.
