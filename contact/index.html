Before you write

  • Questions about your own case (your interview date, your eligibility, a denial): we cannot help with these and will not answer them. Contact USCIS at 800-375-5283 or find free legal help at uscis.gov.
  • Current officials (President, Speaker, your governor): we deliberately do not publish these because they change. Check uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates.
  • Corrections: tell us the question number and the page, and where the official source says otherwise.

Questions and acceptable answers are from the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services 2025 civics test.
