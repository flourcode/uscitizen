Built for a spoken test

The civics test is not a worksheet. A USCIS officer reads each question aloud and you answer in English. The Citizenship Interview Trainer works the same way.

Listen

Each question can be read aloud at the officer's pace, so the wording is familiar before interview day.

Answer out loud

Say your answer before you flip the card. Pulling it from memory is what makes it stick; reading the answer does not.

Swipe honestly

Missed cards return within a few turns. Cards you know wait longer each time. Every 20 cards is scored like the real interview: 12 to pass.

How the 2025 civics test works

20asked at the interview

Which version is yours? If you filed Form N-400 on or after October 20, 2025, you take this 128-question test. If you filed earlier, you take the 2008 version with 100 questions, 10 asked, 6 to pass. The date that matters is your filing date, not your interview date.
65 or older with 20 years as a permanent resident? You study only 20 questions, answer 10, and need 6.

Questions people ask

Which version of the citizenship test will I take?

It depends on when you filed Form N-400, not on your interview date. If you filed on or after October 20, 2025, you take the 2025 test: 128 possible questions, up to 20 asked, 12 correct to pass. If you filed before that date, you take the 2008 test: 100 possible questions, up to 10 asked, 6 correct to pass.

How many questions are on the 2025 civics test?

There are 128 possible questions. During your naturalization interview the officer asks up to 20 of them out loud and stops once you have answered 12 correctly or 9 incorrectly.

Is the civics test multiple choice?

No. It is an oral test. The USCIS officer reads each question aloud and you answer in English. That is why the trainer asks you to say your answer out loud before you flip the card.

What is the 65/20 exception?

If you are 65 or older and have been a lawful permanent resident for 20 or more years, you study only the 20 starred questions, you are asked 10 of them, and you need 6 correct. You may take this version in the language of your choice.

Do I need to memorize every acceptable answer?

No. Most questions accept several answers, and you only need to give as many as the question asks for. Pick the answer you find easiest to say and give that one every time.

Some answers change. How do I know the current President, Speaker, or my governor?

USCIS keeps a list of current officeholders at uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates. Check it the week of your interview and enter your answers in the trainer so you can drill them like everything else.

Is this the official USCIS study app?

No. This is an independent study tool. The questions and acceptable answers are taken from the official USCIS list, but the site is not affiliated with USCIS or any government agency, and nothing here is legal advice.

Does it cost anything or need an account?

No. The trainer is free, needs no account, and stores your progress on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server.
